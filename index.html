🗣️ SPEAKING TASK: SPORTS ORAL PRESENTATION
💡 Sample Model Answer (Example Presentation)
"My favorite sport is volleyball because it is exciting and I enjoy playing with my friends. I usually practice twice a week after school. We play in the school gym, and we need a volleyball and sports shoes. I think volleyball helps me stay healthy because it keeps me active and improves my teamwork. I prefer team sports because I like working with other people. In my opinion, children should play sports every week because exercise is good for their health. In the future, I would like to learn surfing because I love the ocean and I think it looks exciting."

🎙️ Key Expressions

I enjoy... I prefer... I think... In my opinion... I usually... I would like to... I should... because... for example...

Part 1 – Personal Questions

  1. What is your favorite sport? Why?
  2. How often do you play or practice it?
  3. Where do you usually play it?
  4. Who do you usually play with?
  5. What equipment do you need?
  6. How does this sport help you stay healthy?

Part 2 – Comparing Sports

  1. Which is more exciting: playing sports or watching sports?
  2. Which sport is more difficult: tennis or soccer? Why?
  3. Do you prefer team sports or individual sports? Explain your answer.

Part 3 – Giving Opinions

Use because, should, or would like to:

  1. Do you think children should play sports every week? Why?
  2. What sport would you like to learn in the future? Why?
  3. Who is your favorite athlete or sports team? What do you like about them?
